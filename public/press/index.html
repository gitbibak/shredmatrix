Full Balance press kit

Full Balance is a completely free personal workout and nutrition app. It builds a plan for each person's goal, level and the equipment they have, guides every workout set by set, and estimates meal calories from a photo. There is no subscription, no paywall and no ads.

What it does

  • Personal plan: workouts built from the goal, experience level, available days and the equipment declared at signup.
  • Guided workout mode: each exercise with an animated how-to and technique video, set and weight logging, an automatic rest timer and previous weights pre-filled.
  • Nutrition: daily calorie and macro targets that follow the training day, a 7-day menu with budget, health and allergy options.
  • Photo calories: snap a meal, correct foods and portions, and get a calorie range. The photo is processed for the analysis and not stored.
  • Progress: weight, measurements and body-fat trends, reports and Excel export; water and sleep tracking.
  • 28-day challenge: 12 workouts in 28 days, with a printable calendar.

Why it is free

Full Balance is built by an independent founder who wants a complete, honest fitness app to be available to anyone, regardless of budget. Member data is not sold or shared for advertising. The project is kept running by small, optional support from people who use it. Full Balance is not a medical tool and does not replace professional advice.
