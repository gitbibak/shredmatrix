# Full Balance Training Program Quality Audit

Date: 2026-07-24

## Purpose

This document defines how Full Balance training plans should be rebuilt so every module and level feels professional, safe, personalized, and easy to follow.

The current phase labels are:

- Phase 0: Temel / beginner
- Phase 1: Orta / intermediate
- Phase 2: Ileri / advanced
- Phase 3: Usta / expert

The modules are:

- Muscle growth
- Fat loss
- Yoga
- Pilates
- Reformer
- Meditation

## Evidence Base

The redesign should follow these principles:

- ACSM 2026 resistance training position stand: consistency, effort, individualization and training all major muscle groups at least twice per week are more important than unnecessary complexity. Strength outcomes favor heavier loading around 80% 1RM, 2-3 sets per exercise; hypertrophy benefits from weekly volume around 10 sets per muscle group, with diminishing returns above high volumes. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/
- ACSM 2026 public summary: the best program is the one users can maintain; advanced methods like failure, complex periodization, or special equipment are optional for most users. Source: https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/
- WHO 2020 guidelines: adults should aim for 150-300 min moderate aerobic activity or 75-150 min vigorous activity weekly, plus muscle strengthening for all major muscle groups on 2+ days/week. Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK566046/
- ACSM preparticipation screening: people with signs/symptoms or known cardiovascular/metabolic/renal disease need medical clearance before starting or increasing intensity; otherwise exercise should progress gradually. Source: https://journals.lww.com/acsm-msse/fulltext/2015/11000/updating_acsm_s_recommendations_for_exercise.28.aspx
- Yoga safety evidence: yoga is generally safe when performed properly, but beginners should avoid extreme practices such as headstands, shoulder stands, lotus and forceful breathing; musculoskeletal sprains/strains are the most common adverse events. Source: https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/yoga-effectiveness-and-safety and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28958637/
- Pilates evidence: Pilates can improve flexibility, dynamic balance and muscular endurance in healthy users; for low back pain, 2-3 sessions/week around 50 minutes for 8-12 weeks is supported in recent reviews. Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22030232/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41178988/
- Injury prevention evidence: adherence is a major driver of injury-prevention benefit; programs should be realistic enough that users actually complete them. Source: https://injuryprevention.bmj.com/content/early/2025/08/05/ip-2025-045632

## Current Problems Found

1. Phase names are better than the actual progression.
   Some advanced and expert phases look intense, but they do not always show a clean progression in weekly volume, intensity, recovery and skill prerequisites.

2. Too many expert plans rely on flashy exercises.
   Advanced techniques, supersets, dropsets, handstands, full repertoires and long flows appear before the app has verified readiness, mobility, symptoms, equipment or consistency.

3. Health filtering exists, but the program templates should be safer by default.
   The generator now replaces risky exercises for selected conditions, but the base templates still include movements that are unnecessarily aggressive for many users.

4. Fat-loss programming mixes conditioning and lifting without enough fatigue control.
   HIIT should not be the default answer for every high-level fat-loss plan. Fat loss should prioritize adherence, strength retention, steps/cardio dose and recovery.

5. Yoga/Pilates/Reformer levels are skill ladders, not just harder names.
   Beginner should teach foundations. Intermediate should add complexity. Advanced should add intensity/range only if safe. Expert should still have regression options.

6. Meditation is not handled like a structured practice.
   It needs duration, technique, difficulty, nervous-system goal, and progression. It should not look like a random list of sessions.

## New Quality Standard

Every generated workout day must have:

- Clear goal: strength, hypertrophy, conditioning, mobility, skill, recovery, or breathwork.
- Difficulty tag: beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert.
- Expected duration.
- Warm-up or preparation block.
- Main work block.
- Accessory or skill block when relevant.
- Cooldown or recovery instruction.
- Progression rule.
- Regression option.
- Health-condition guardrails.

Every weekly plan must have:

- 2+ major muscle strengthening exposures when applicable.
- At least 1-2 recovery or low-intensity days depending on level.
- No repeated maximal spinal loading on consecutive days.
- No consecutive high-impact HIIT days.
- Health-aware exercise substitution.
- Level-appropriate volume and complexity.
- A clear reason why each day exists.

## Module Redesign

### Muscle Growth

Goal: hypertrophy with strength foundation, not random bodybuilding volume.

Phase 0 - Temel:

- 3-4 days/week.
- Full body or upper/lower.
- Main compounds first, machine/bodyweight options available.
- 2-3 sets per exercise.
- RPE 6-7.
- No failure, no dropsets.

Phase 1 - Orta:

- 4-5 days/week.
- Upper/lower or push/pull/legs plus full-body touch.
- 8-12 weekly hard sets per major muscle.
- RPE 7-8.
- Direct arm/delt/core volume controlled.

Phase 2 - Ileri:

- 5-6 days/week only if recovery inputs are acceptable.
- PPL or upper/lower hybrid.
- 10-16 weekly sets per target muscle.
- Top-set/back-off or double progression.
- Deload every 4-6 weeks.

Phase 3 - Usta:

- 5-6 days/week, not 7 hard days.
- Block periodization: accumulation, intensification, deload.
- Advanced methods limited to 1-2 exercises per session.
- Explicit readiness checks: sleep, soreness, missed workouts.

### Fat Loss

Goal: retain muscle, improve conditioning, increase weekly energy output.

Phase 0 - Temel:

- 3 strength sessions/week.
- 2 low-intensity cardio/step days.
- No aggressive HIIT.

Phase 1 - Orta:

- 3-4 strength sessions.
- 2-3 cardio sessions.
- 1 optional short interval session.

Phase 2 - Ileri:

- 4 strength sessions.
- 2 LISS sessions.
- 1 HIIT/circuit session maximum.
- Fatigue scoring required.

Phase 3 - Usta:

- Hybrid plan with strength retention as the anchor.
- Conditioning waves, not random daily intensity.
- Deload every 4 weeks or when recovery drops.

### Yoga

Goal: mobility, strength, balance, breath control and safe skill progression.

Phase 0 - Temel:

- Foundational poses, breath awareness, low-risk mobility.
- Avoid headstand, shoulder stand, lotus, deep backbend and forceful breathing.

Phase 1 - Orta:

- Longer flows, simple balance work, controlled hip/hamstring mobility.
- Intro to inversion preparation only.

Phase 2 - Ileri:

- Skill work only with regression paths.
- Backbends/inversions introduced as optional progressions, not mandatory.

Phase 3 - Usta:

- Advanced flow, pranayama and inversions, but every pose needs prerequisite and contraindication notes.

### Pilates

Goal: core control, spinal articulation, posture, balance and muscular endurance.

Phase 0 - Temel:

- 2-3 sessions/week.
- Breathing, neutral spine, pelvic control, low-load core.

Phase 1 - Orta:

- 3 sessions/week.
- More unilateral, anti-rotation, dynamic balance.

Phase 2 - Ileri:

- Full mat sequencing, higher endurance, controlled transitions.

Phase 3 - Usta:

- Long flow and choreography only after the user has completed prior blocks.
- Include regressions for neck/back/wrist discomfort.

### Reformer

Goal: equipment-guided control, spring resistance, core, mobility and joint-friendly strength.

Phase 0 - Temel:

- Setup education, spring control, footwork, basic straps.
- No unstable advanced positions.

Phase 1 - Orta:

- More unilateral leg work, rowing, box work.
- Tempo and range added gradually.

Phase 2 - Ileri:

- Jump board and long box only if knee/back flags are clear.
- Volume capped by session duration.

Phase 3 - Usta:

- Tower/Cadillac-style references should be separated from reformer-only plans unless equipment is selected.
- Add equipment availability question later.

### Meditation

Goal: nervous-system regulation and adherence, not “exercise list” formatting.

Phase 0 - Temel:

- 5-10 minutes.
- Breath counting, body scan, simple awareness.

Phase 1 - Orta:

- 10-20 minutes.
- Box breathing, compassion practice, focused attention.

Phase 2 - Ileri:

- 20-30 minutes.
- Open monitoring, longer body scan, walking meditation.

Phase 3 - Usta:

- 30-45 minutes.
- Longer sits and breath retention only with clear safety warnings.

## Implementation Plan

1. Replace static `workoutPhases` with structured phase templates.
   Each phase should include `duration`, `goal`, `intensity`, `progression`, `regression`, and `safetyNotes`.

2. Add a quality validator.
   The validator should fail generated plans that have:
   - too many hard days,
   - missing rest,
   - unsafe yoga inversions for beginners,
   - repeated high spinal-load days,
   - HIIT overload,
   - no progression rule,
   - no health-aware substitutions.

3. Add module-specific readiness logic.
   The generator should adjust plans using:
   - experience,
   - age,
   - health conditions,
   - sleep,
   - recent workout adherence,
   - equipment availability later.

4. Improve UI wording.
   Training cards should show why the day exists:
   - "Bugun kuvvet temeli"
   - "Bugun toparlanma"
   - "Bugun dusuk yogunluk kardiyo"
   - "Bugun mobilite + core"

5. Add tests per module.
   Tests should cover:
   - beginner yoga has no inversions,
   - fat-loss phase has no back-to-back HIIT,
   - muscle plans hit major muscle groups 2x/week,
   - health flags replace risky exercises,
   - meditation phases progress by duration.

## Decision

The current system should not be improved by only adding more exercises. It needs a stronger program engine:

- evidence-based phase rules,
- safer defaults,
- personalized substitutions,
- measurable weekly volume,
- recovery-aware progression,
- and tests that prevent low-quality templates from returning.

This is the standard the next implementation should target.
