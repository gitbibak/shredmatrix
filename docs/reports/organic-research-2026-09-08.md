## Channel priorities

1. Preserve the converting English home entry. Main positioning:
   free personal home workout AND nutrition plan, with/without equipment.
   Primary evidence: Full Balance's own attributed registrations.
2. Pinterest organic: first diversification experiment, not proven best channel.
   Use actual app screenshots and a saveable weekly-plan preview, not another
   six-feature poster. EN and ES get matching destination pages and distinct UTM.
3. Google: optimize existing pages only after query/page evidence is available.
   Avoid multiplying near-duplicate SEO pages or rewriting /en every day.
4. Pilates: next topic candidate. Public results emphasize short follow-along
   sessions, beginner modifications, weekly calendars and video. A generic
   feature list does not meet that intent as well as a usable sample.
5. Reformer: narrower equipment-specific audience. Keep clearly separate from
   no-equipment mat Pilates; no invented safe spring settings or expert claims.
6. Meditation: do not promise an audio experience unless the product delivers it.
   Reassess public query results and actual landing conversions before expansion.
7. Communities and creators: answer a real question, disclose founder affiliation,
   obey each community's self-promotion rules. No automated bulk replies or DMs.

## Competitor lessons, not copied claims

- [FitFinder](https://fitfinder.es/) foregrounds workout/nutrition, equipment,
  no-registration use and PDF. Lesson: demonstrate the output early. Do not claim
  Full Balance has the same no-account flow without testing it.
- [NourishYou](https://nourishyou.ca/app.html) shows concrete meal/workout output,
  a short quiz and coach credentials. Lesson: real product proof beats a large
  module list. Never invent credentials for Full Balance.
- [Nourish Move Love](https://www.nourishmovelove.com/beginner-pilates-workout/)
  offers a specific 15-minute beginner session. Lesson: duration and demonstrable
  content align with the beginner Pilates search intent.
- [GymiaFit](https://gymia.fit/) markets workout plans plus nutrition and many
  modules. Feature count alone is not a durable differentiator.

## First distribution experiment: prepared, not published

Reuse the EN/ES copy and UTM links in growth-check-2026-09-07.md.
One hypothesis: an actual weekly-plan preview attracts more qualified visitors
than the existing six-goal poster.

Creative: 2:3 portrait; one clear headline, legible real weekly-plan screenshot,
short free/no-card line and brand. No fake testimonials or body transformations.
EN headline: Free Home Workout + Nutrition Plan.
ES headline: Tu plan gratis de entrenamiento y nutricion en casa.
Pin description must match the destination and name equipment options.
Do not state exact photo calories, unlimited scans, guaranteed fat loss or results.

Evaluation starts on actual posting, not the date this report was written.
Wait at least 3 completed days; if fewer than 30 measured outbound clicks arrive,
extend to 7-14 days instead of declaring failure. This is an operational rule,
not a statistical significance threshold.
Primary outcome: attributed new members who create a plan; secondary: D1/D7.
Record outbound clicks from the platform separately from consented site sessions.
No clicks: inspect creative/topic distribution. Clicks but no registrations:
inspect destination and auth friction. Registrations but no return:
prioritize the first workout and next-day value, not more acquisition copy.
